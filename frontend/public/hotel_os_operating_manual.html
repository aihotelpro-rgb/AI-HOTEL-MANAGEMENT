03

1-Click Check-In, Check-Out & GST Billing

⚡ Express 1-Click Check-In SOP

  1. Open Stayview or Today's Arrivals tab.
  2. Locate guest booking and click ⚡ 1-Click Convert to Check-In.
  3. Verify guest photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport) and nationality for C-Form compliance.
  4. Record Advance Payment if collected (Cash, Card, UPI QR, or Bank Transfer).
  5. Click Confirm Check-In: Room automatically turns Occupied, resident profile links, and digital keycard SMS/WhatsApp is sent to the guest.

💳 Check-Out & GST Settlement SOP

  1. Click Check-Out on the resident's suite card or Stayview row.
  2. The system compiles the Consolidated Guest Bill:
    • Room Accommodation charges (Nights × Rate)
    • In-Room Dining KOT charges from Kitchen POS
    • 12% GST breakdown (6% CGST + 6% SGST)
    • Deduction of previously recorded advance deposits
  3. Collect remaining balance via Cash / Card Swipe / UPI QR.
  4. Click Settle Bill & Check Out: System prints official GST Tax Invoice, marks room Turnover (Dirty), and moves record to Past Guest Stay History.

05

Bedside QR Dining, Kitchen KDS & Runner Delivery

📱

1. Guest Scans Acrylic Standee

Each suite features a unique Bedside QR Standee. The resident scans it with their phone camera to view the live digital kitchen menu with photos, spice ratings, and preparation times. No mobile app download required.

👨‍🍳

2. Kitchen Display System (KDS)

Orders ring directly inside the kitchen on the touch-enabled KDS screen with audio chime alerts. The Chef updates statuses: Pending → Preparing → Ready for Pickup. Charges auto-post to the room bill.

🛵

3. Room Runner Dispatch

Service staff (Runners) view ready trays on their mobile handhelds. When picked up, they mark Out for Delivery, serve the suite, and click Delivered. The guest receives a WhatsApp confirmation.

06

Travel Agent Master & B2B Credit Ledger

Essential for tourism markets (such as Andaman DMCs & tour operators). Travel agents operate on special voucher rates and scheduled credit settlements rather than retail walk-in prices.

Model A

100% Full Advance Pre-Paid

Agency transfers total room rent via NEFT/UPI prior to arrival. Front desk enters Bank UTR. Room rent folio shows ₹0 balance; guest only pays for personal extras.

Model B

Part Advance + Desk Balance

Agency deposits 30%–50% advance token. The remaining balance is collected directly from the guest or agency at front-desk check-in.

Model C

Bill-to-Company (Credit Ledger)

Contracted agencies receive an authorized Credit Limit (e.g. ₹2,50,000) and 15/30-day payment cycle. Stays auto-debit the agent's ledger for periodic bulk settlement.

08

Night Audit, DSR & Financial Reporting

At the end of every operating day (typically 23:30 hrs), the General Manager or Night Auditor opens the Daily Sales Report (DSR) modal. The system automatically reconciles:

Occupancy Rate 87.5%
ADR (Avg Daily Rate) ₹4,850
RevPAR ₹4,243
Cash in Drawer ₹38,200

The DSR report compiles Room Revenue, Food & Beverage revenue, GST collection, and Cashier Settlement breakdown (Cash vs POS Card Swipe vs UPI QR vs Bank Transfer) and can be printed in 1 click for hotel ownership.

Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) Shift Checklists

🌅 Morning Shift (07:00 - 15:30)

  • Review Today's Expected Departures in Stayview.
  • Notify Kitchen of breakfast buffet guest counts by meal plan (CP, MAP).
  • Process guest checkouts & print final GST tax invoices.
  • Assign checked-out rooms to Housekeeping for turnover cleaning.

☀️ Afternoon Shift (14:00 - 22:30)

  • Inspect cleaned suites and mark status Clean on Room Grid.
  • Execute 1-Click Check-In for arriving flights and ferry groups.
  • Validate Tour Operator vouchers and meal plans (MAP dinner covers).
  • Monitor live Intercom and WhatsApp feed for room service requests.

🌙 Night Audit (22:00 - 07:30)

  • Audit in-house active stays against physical key slots.
  • Reconcile cashier drawer cash against card swipe & UPI settlement slips.
  • Generate and print Daily DSR Report.
  • Pre-assign clean suites for tomorrow's early morning check-ins.

Emergency, Intercom & Incident Protocols

🚨 Front Desk Two-Way Intercom Console

The system includes a browser-based, hardware-free WebRTC Audio Intercom connecting the front desk directly to guest smartphones and in-room digital tablets.

Inbound Emergency Call

When a guest presses "Front Desk Call" on their room QR, the header flashes green with audio chime. Click Answer Call to speak with the resident immediately.

Outbound Wakeup & Courtesy Call

To contact any occupied suite for wake-up calls or cab arrival, select the room from the Intercom modal and click Dial Suite.
